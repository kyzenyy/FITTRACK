Run FitTrack on Android & iPhone (iOS)

Universal Progressive Web App (PWA) • Offline Ready

Direct One-Tap Install Available!

Your browser supports installing FitTrack directly to your app drawer.

Step 1: Get Your Universal App File Works on Both OS

Unlike .apk files (which only work on Android and are blocked on iPhones), this single universal FitTrack_App.html file runs natively on both Android and iOS with offline storage.

🍎 iPhone / iOS (Safari)
  1. Open the app link or file in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share icon ↑ at the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch FitTrack from your Home Screen in full-screen app mode!
🤖 Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the app link or file in Chrome on Android.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu ⋮ in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. FitTrack installs onto your phone alongside your native apps!
